Cybercrime Reporting Resources

Detected spam or toxic content is not itself an official cybercrime report. If you believe you are a victim, use the appropriate official channel below.

1. Cybercrime Portal — Online abuse and cybercrime

Report online harassment, cyberbullying, threats, impersonation, and phishing-related incidents.

Official Government of India portal

Report cybercrime online ↗

2. Cybercrime helpline — 1930

Call 1930 if you have suffered financial cyber fraud, such as losing money to a phishing link or scam. Report the incident as quickly as possible.

For immediate danger, contact police or call 112.

3. Sanchar Saathi / Chakshu — Suspicious calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages

Use Chakshu to report suspected fraud communications, impersonation, and certain malicious messages received through calls, SMS, or WhatsApp.

Open Sanchar Saathi ↗
